# /brag plan: Whiteboard Sensei

## The answers

- **What is it:** A live AI tutor you talk to that teaches on a shared whiteboard, drawing and explaining step by step.
- **Who is it for:** Students learning anything (math, languages, physics, chemistry, geography, history).
- **What sets it apart:** It shows the work instead of just talking. Precise visuals come from computation or real data rather than the model guessing: computed geometry, real kanji stroke order (KanjiVG), real map borders (Natural Earth), real molecules (PubChem), charts to scale, and openly licensed photos credited automatically.
- **Most impressive claim:** The kanji 新 drawn in its real 13-stroke order, live, while the tutor explains each stroke.
- **Visual hook:** A triangle drawing itself on a dark board, under the line "AI tutors talk. This one draws."
- **Real UI / flow shown:** The app mid-lesson (board on the left, the tutor's spoken lines in the panel), then the board drawing: triangle, kanji strokes, a map with trade routes, caffeine's structure, a bar chart and a credited photo. All footage is the actual app running its built-in lesson.
- **Tone:** `default`: punchy, playful and clean, matched to the app's dark neon look (violet → blue → teal → amber).
- **Share caption:** "Most AI tutors just talk. Whiteboard Sensei draws: real stroke order, real maps, real molecules, live on a whiteboard while it explains."

Privacy: no secrets, user data or internal URLs appear anywhere. The panel's status reads "Speaking…" during the demo footage, as it would in a live session; that line is illustrative.
